Why Indian Holiday?

Indian Holiday Pvt. Ltd. - Your Holiday Tour Planner in India & Around the World

Dominating the landscape of South Asia, India is home to millions of attractions and billions of experiences. Blessed with varied geographical features, India has a vast diversity of hills, plateaus, beaches, backwaters, islands, forests, deserts, and more. Be a part of yoga retreats, go for pilgrimages, or take up Ayurveda therapies or spa sessions. A holiday trip across India is worth every penny.

How Indian Holiday designs the ideal holiday for you:

With an industry experience of over 34 years, we bring you the best of magnificent India through more than 600 tours! A tireless team of over 100 travel experts is invested in these tours. We provide a wide range of exciting themes and packages, such as family vacations, honeymoon trips, adventure tours, etc., to popular locations in India, Nepal, and Bhutan. We give you, the guest, the power to select or create your itinerary. You can choose any of our India tours OR add your inputs to the existing tours and create a fresh, brand new tour yourself, tailor-made to your needs.

IHPL – The Website

This website is a virtual encyclopedia on anything and everything to be seen, visited, tasted, tried, enjoyed, partaken, captured, or admired in India. Be it a place, attraction, food, festival, activity, hotel, or restaurant, you will find it within the website. IndianHoliday.com features exhaustive information about all possible topics and categories for a tour in India. All of the enclosed information has been carefully segmented under relevant headers. Users can easily access and browse popular destinations, holiday themes, expert recommendations, and our services from the homepage itself. Read the Company section if you want to know more about Indian Holiday. Make good use of the search bar at the top. We assure you will not need to go to another website.

How to Book Your Holidays?

Booking a tour at IndianHoliday.com is quick, simple, and effective. Most of our pages feature an elaborate inquiry form where you can mention your email, list some basic tour specifics or even jot down a question. That's it! Our tour expert promptly reverts to your inquiry, and within no time, your tour to India is booked.
